## Summary

AISLOPOPEDIA is a satirical living catalogue of 217 phrases and templates arranged into 26 labelled groups across nine content sections, followed by an equal-weight five-band self-score and an AI-voiced confession. It is useful for discovering social-post and promotional-writing candidates, especially hooks, pivots, closers, comments, emoji, corporate vocabulary, and fill-in-the-blank structures. It is not an empirical study: it supplies no model version, corpus, human comparison, frequencies, annotation procedure, or validation, so its examples can inform matched evaluation and rewrite questions but cannot validate a threshold, prevalence claim, causal mechanism, authorship score, or document verdict.

## Main insights

- The 26 catalogue families cover throat-clearers, false exclusivity, urgency, binary contrast, numbered hooks, fragmentation, performed vulnerability, false agency, escalation ladders, pivots, qualifier sandwiches, calls to action, generic closers, mic drops, agreement and engagement comments, credential prefaces, emoji placement, AI-post wrappers, time compression, artificial scarcity, adverbs, adjectives, verbs, and sentence templates.
- Current isolated-example coverage is uneven. The live 50-check deterministic registry produced at least one failing result for 28 of 217 instantiated examples. Existing checks are strongest on selected manufactured-insight, negative-parallelism, staccato, anaphora, significance, promotional, and performed-candour surfaces.
- Unicode coverage is intentionally thresholded: 18 isolated source symbols or symbol-bearing examples produced candidate matches without failing the two-candidate aggregate. The current symbol set misses the source's ⚡, ➡️, and ♻️ forms.
- The page's own categorical labels, three-adverb authorship claim, equal-weight self-score, “written by an AI” framing, training explanation, and phrase-origin story are unsupported page assertions or satire, not evidence that any phrase proves authorship.
- The source provides no null experiment, measured negative result, counterbalanced human sample, or validated rewrite outcome. Its important counterweight is therefore the evidence boundary itself: the phrases are candidate stylistic constructions, many of them ordinary human rhetoric, and require context, genre, frequency, placement, quotation, deliberate-use, and matched-human controls.
