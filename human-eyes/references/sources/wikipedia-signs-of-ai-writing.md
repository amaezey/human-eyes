## Summary

The source is a living, Wikipedia-specific catalogue of writing, formatting, markup, citation, comment, and editor-behaviour patterns assembled from selected observed edits and outside research. This refresh preserves the complete main page at revision 1364308809 and the complete companion comment catalogue at revision 1364289488, including every rendered section, example, table, note, reference, and the one content-bearing screenshot. It is useful for candidate discovery, contextual controls, dated model differences, and source-integrity review, but it is not a systematic corpus, validated detector, prevalence estimate, causal study, or basis for authorship claims. Relative to the prior card-reviewed revisions, the substantive catalogue is almost unchanged: one new challenges example and `functions as` / `operates as` wording were added, while other changes corrected links or template markup.

## Main insights

- The page repeatedly states that its catalogue is descriptive, Wikipedia-specific, and insufficient for proving AI use; signs should prompt review of deeper sourcing, policy, and provenance problems rather than cosmetic removal.
- Its strongest contribution is breadth: content inflation, lexical and syntactic habits, formatting, platform residue, citation failures, comment scripts, edit-summary behaviour, model drift, human look-alikes, and dated historical indicators appear in one source.
- The evidence is heterogeneous. Selected Wikipedia diffs are direct catalogue examples; most generalisation, frequency, model-family, and detection-performance claims are inherited from cited sources and remain indirect here.
- The source itself supplies important controls: humans use em dashes and curly punctuation; professional or mixed-register prose, letters, transition words, unsourced text, and correct or bizarre wikitext are ineffective alone; category mistakes and abrupt cutoffs have human or tooling explanations.
- Vocabulary and model behaviour are explicitly time-sensitive. The page distinguishes GPT-4, GPT-4o, GPT-5/5.1, Grok, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, and Perplexity observations rather than presenting one timeless AI style.
- The source challenges current any-occurrence treatment of em dashes and curly quotation marks, isolated `in order to`, and flat low-vocabulary-diversity assumptions.
- Several direct residue families are stronger provenance candidates than prose style: `turn0search0`, `contentReference`, `oaicite`, `attributableIndex`, `:::writing`, `attached_file`, Grok citation JSON/tags, DeepSeek brackets, and Gemini cite markers.
- Citation and source-integrity failures include dead or invented links, invalid or unrelated DOIs/ISBNs, incomplete book citations, malformed reference use, tracking parameters, and unused named references; the source also lists non-existent categories, templates, shortcuts, policies, and maintenance markup.
- Comment-specific evidence is composite and context-bound. It combines assurances, offers to revise, source-assessment requests, policy scripts, speculation complaints, sectioning, subject lines, transclusions, sealioning, and wikilawyering; it should not become one phrase blacklist.
- Behavioural observations such as permissions gaming, edit-history style shifts, and explanations of editorial choices are provenance context, not sentence-level prose checks.
- The page's positive-human and ineffective-indicator sections are as important as its candidate list: old dates, explainable mistakes, direct statements, individual hedges, wordy constructions, professional grammar, and established correspondence formats can counter a superficial accusation.
- The complete source materially strengthens provenance and context mapping but produces a moderate-to-small change in product understanding because most surface families were already catalogued and all recommendations remain pending.

## Evidence and claims to extract

Scope is English Wikipedia informational writing, drafts, comments, edit summaries, user pages, and platform-specific markup.
- **Direct versus cited evidence:** The examples, page structure, explicit caveats, and editor-catalogue descriptions are direct. Detection rates, corpus trends, model comparisons, overuse claims, and causal explanations attributed to references are indirect in this record unless a separately reviewed source card exists. No inherited number or model-general claim is promoted from Wikipedia alone.
- **Important limits and counterexamples:** The page is an advice page rather than policy; many examples are selected because editors already suspected AI; several indicators are explicitly rare, historical, platform-specific, discussion-page-heavy, or useful only in combination. Human typography, genre, professional editing, language education, non-native English use, code switching, tool bugs, old categories, link rot, and ordinary copy/paste errors are named look-alikes. Surface patterns do not prove authorship.

## Skill-use audit

- **Good use:** Candidate discovery; dated model and platform provenance; controls for frequency, genre, formatting, quotation, history, and human look-alikes; manual source/citation checks; regression fixtures for exact residue strings.
- **Misuse / overclaim:** Treating selected Wikipedia examples as prevalence, causal or model-general evidence; using any single word, punctuation mark, error, or detector score as proof; importing cited findings without reading their direct sources.
- **Unsupported use:** Authorship classification, universal thresholds, current-model prevalence, non-Wikipedia genre claims, causal training/RLHF mechanisms, or claims that cosmetic rewriting resolves the underlying source and policy problems.
- **Underused evidence:** Direct interface residue, model/date metadata, citation-integrity review, positive-human evidence, ineffective-indicator controls, and composite comment/profile/edit-summary structures.
- **Patterns left on the table:** Proper-noun list leads, small-table misuse, skipped heading levels, thematic breaks, stiff lexical substitutions, source-type notability enumeration, `X rather than Y`, exact citation wrappers, edit-summary composites, pre-placed maintenance templates, and behaviour/history comparisons.
