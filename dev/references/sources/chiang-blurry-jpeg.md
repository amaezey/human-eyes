## Summary

Chiang's February 2023 essay uses lossy compression as an extended analogy for early ChatGPT: fluent rephrasing can preserve gist while concealing factual error, and generated boilerplate can bypass the writer's own discovery and revision process. The recovered complete article adds the 2013 Xerox incident, Chiang's Hutter Prize and anti-anthropomorphism discussion, and his explicit use-case framing that the earlier 22-paragraph snapshot omitted. This remains interpretive practitioner evidence rather than an LLM experiment or prose-pattern corpus.
