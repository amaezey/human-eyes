## Main insights

- Preston describes conversational AI as a heterogeneous field rather than a synonym for ChatGPT and argues that a short designated-contrarian slot pressures criticism toward abstractions.
- She reports visual and rhetorical convergence across conference booths and presentations, but gives no coding method, denominator, comparison event, or prevalence measure.
- A Journey presenter used a ChatGPT-powered tool to produce a hypothetical cereal package and the exact copy `Are you tired of starting your day with a bland and boring breakfast? Look no further than PureCrunch Naturals.` The article supplies no prompt, model version, full output, or repeated trials.
- Other system/demo outputs include Canary's score report, Cathy's health-care promotion and doctor disclaimer, an in-home medication reminder, and VERA dialogue embedded in a CEO's story. Only PureCrunch is explicitly described as ChatGPT-powered marketing copy; the other outputs have distinct or uncertain scripting, model, and verification status.
- Chatty, Chief, Cathy, and VERA illustrate ambiguous or inflated roles, synthetic persona design, unreliable demos, and a gap between branding and demonstrated capability.
- Vendor speakers proposed elder-care avatars, synthetic doubles, vocal-biomarker screening, mental-health relief, and frictionless hospitality. These are reported claims and pitches, not independently verified findings.
- The article preserves counterquestions and nulls: poor audio, cultural differences, generic baselines, outliers, employee surveillance, follow-up after veterinary advice, nonfunctional wi-fi, inconsistent product descriptions, and demonstrations that could not be shown.
- Preston retells the Brenda/Ella episode from her earlier work: a Spanish-speaking tenant's urgent messages, pronoun/name error, inaccessible landlord, and human operators without authority. This is first-person retrospective evidence, not an independent replication of `HUMAN_FALLBACK`.
- She distinguishes declared good intentions from implementations that may isolate, extract, surveil, defraud, or redirect responsibility.
- Her `collapse of context` example concerns a human conference speech that she calls ChatGPT-like. It is a human look-alike and interpretive analogy, not evidence that a phrase proves AI authorship.
- Her claim that LLM summaries smooth eccentric style into authoritative mediocrity is an author interpretation without a source-versus-summary comparison.
- Conference claims that conversation cures loneliness are directly countered by Preston's own experience of loneliness in conversation and connection beyond language.
- The VERA euthanasia episode is a CEO's story reported by Preston; no user record, diagnosis, follow-up, or outcome verification appears.
- Preston observes repeated ethics language and diversity proposals, but argues that intentions and representation do not resolve extractive design or foreseeable misuse.
- The essay explicitly supplies negative capability evidence: Preston was not convinced the showcased technologies could hasten societal collapse because some could not connect to the internet.
- She argues that institutionalized criticism can become benediction and that workers may fall back into a rehearsed futurist script despite private weariness or doubt.
- A Holocaust-survivor-clone presentation pivots from museum education to brand representation. This is one reported dual-use example, not a general deployment rate.
