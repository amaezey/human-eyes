## Summary

ZeroGPT's living vendor page markets a text-origin classifier, describes a paste/upload-to-percentage workflow, names broad model, content, technique, and use-case coverage, and adds nine FAQ answers about reliability, privacy, supported files, and account limits. The full 2026-07-17 capture is useful for product-landscape contrast and for its own explicit statement that machine-learning predictions are probabilistic and should be combined with human judgment. It is not independent evaluation or prose-pattern evidence: the page supplies a `98.5%` headline, broad feature and corpus labels, and a `200K+` user count without a detector build, benchmark, threshold, calibration, error analysis, subgroup results, or reproducible method. Its image-level `100%` gauge, `100% free` and `Unlimited` copy, premium limits, and rewrite-to-`undetectable` advice require especially careful boundaries.

## Main insights

- The page repeatedly advertises `98.5%` accuracy and `200K+` users, while one preserved illustration displays a `100%` gauge. No evaluation data, detector version, threshold, calibration, uncertainty, or independent validation supports those figures on this page.
- ZeroGPT names sentence structure, word choice, coherence, tone, meaning, organization, patterns, NLP, machine learning, neural networks, and a large mixed AI/human training corpus. These are opaque feature or method families, not operational definitions, ablations, directions, weights, rates, or manual rules that human-eyes can adopt.
- The FAQ says no AI detector is 100% accurate, calls its predictions probabilistic, and recommends combining automation with human judgment. This qualification limits the page's stronger `written by a human or generated by an AI` wording but does not establish score calibration or safe high-consequence use.
- The page conflates AI-origin classification with originality, authenticity, plagiarism, misinformation, academic integrity, spam, and SEO, and explicitly recommends editing or humanizing text to make it `undetectable AI`. Those are different evidence categories and the rewrite-to-score workflow can encourage camouflage and meaning drift.
- The only concrete input boundaries are the 5,000-character free limit, 50,000-character premium limit, batch processing, and named file formats. The page gives no minimum length or length-performance curve.
- The refreshed capture recovers nine complete FAQ answers absent from the prior Jina snapshot. The current canonical copy also differs from that prior extraction in two technology paragraphs and six of seven purpose descriptions; the seventh, `Easy to use`, is unchanged. The model and headline claims remain materially similar. The comparison documents capture and copy differences without claiming when the FAQ text appeared or treating wording changes as changed product performance.
