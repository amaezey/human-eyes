## Summary

AISLOPOPEDIA is a satirical living catalogue of 217 phrases and templates arranged into 26 labelled groups across nine content sections, followed by an equal-weight five-band self-score and an AI-voiced confession. It is useful for discovering social-post and promotional-writing candidates, especially hooks, pivots, closers, comments, emoji, corporate vocabulary, and fill-in-the-blank structures. It is not an empirical study: it supplies no model version, corpus, human comparison, frequencies, annotation procedure, or validation, so its examples can inform matched evaluation and rewrite questions but cannot validate a threshold, prevalence claim, causal mechanism, authorship score, or document verdict.
