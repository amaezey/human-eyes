## FAQs on our AI Detector

Everything you need to know about ZeroGPT

### What is an AI Detection Tool?

An AI detection tool — often searched as 'Zero GPT' or 'ChatGPT Zero' — analyzes text to determine whether it was generated by an AI like ChatGPT, DeepSeek, or Gemini, or written by a human. ZeroGPT examines how the text is organized, its sentence structures, and its writing patterns to make this determination, helping protect content authenticity.

### How to Use ZeroGPT?

Using ZeroGPT is simple: paste your text into the box on the page and click the 'Detect Origin' button. Within seconds, you'll see whether the text was written by a human or generated by an AI model, with a sentence-by-sentence breakdown.

### Which AI models does ZeroGPT detect?

ZeroGPT can accurately detect content from all major AI language models including ChatGPT (GPT-3.5 and GPT-5), Claude (all versions), Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama, Mistral, Bard, and other AI writing tools. Our detection technology is continuously updated to recognize new AI models as they are released.

### How does AI detector detect ChatGPT or AI-Plagiarized content?

Our AI detector uses NLP techniques and multiple signals to detect AI-generated or ChatGPT text. It analyzes sentence structure, word choice, and coherence to identify AI patterns. ZeroGPT's detector learned to differentiate between AI and human content by training on large, diverse datasets of both.

### Is the AI detector reliable?

No AI detector is 100% accurate — machine learning predictions are probabilistic by nature. Accuracy improves with continuous development and training on how words, patterns, tone, and meaning are used. ZeroGPT combines NLP techniques with additional signals and continuous retraining, which is why it delivers among the most reliable results available; we still recommend combining automated detection with human judgment.

### Is my text kept private and secure?

Absolutely! We take your privacy seriously. ZeroGPT never stores your submitted text or uses it for training purposes. Your content is analyzed in real-time and immediately discarded after detection. We are GDPR compliant and use enterprise-grade encryption to protect all data transmission.

### How accurate is ZeroGPT?

ZeroGPT is known for accurately recognizing whether content was generated by AI models like ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude, and Gemini or written by a human. While no AI detection is perfect, ZeroGPT continually improves its accuracy by training on large datasets and analyzing word use, patterns, and tone. It's a free, reliable tool for distinguishing AI-generated from human-written content.

### What file formats does ZeroGPT support?

ZeroGPT supports direct text input (copy-paste), .txt files, .docx (Microsoft Word), .pdf documents, and .odt files. You can upload documents up to 5,000 characters for free accounts. Premium users can analyze files up to 50,000 characters and benefit from batch file processing.
