# Pilot additions prompt leakage audit

The prompts retain only document genre, audience or setting, communicative purpose, broad topic, approximate length, and ordinary privacy or factual safeguards. Where an email or report needs concrete inputs, a short fictional brief supplies facts unrelated to the paired human document’s facts and sequence.

## Cross-prompt review

- No prompt includes text, names, distinctive facts, incidents, argument order, section order, or project elements from its paired human sample.
- No prompt prescribes paragraph count, heading pattern, bullet count, salutation, signoff, sentence completeness, or the comparison document’s organization.
- No prompt requests naturalness, human-likeness, a distinctive voice, awkwardness, repetition, paragraph variation, imitation, or deliberate imperfection.
- No prompt names the benchmark, a detector, a prose tendency, a scoring rule, or an avoidance strategy.
- Approximate length remains because document length is an ordinary commissioning constraint and the evaluation requires comparable opportunities for findings.
- Fictional briefs provide enough substance for professional and institutional tasks without asking the generator to reproduce human-source facts or fabricate claims about real organizations.
