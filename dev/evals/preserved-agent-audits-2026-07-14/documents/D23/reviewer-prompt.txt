Hello everyone,

We are preparing to launch a three-month pilot program designed to give our volunteer technical specialists more consistent support. Before we finalise the arrangements, we would appreciate feedback from volunteers, coordinators, and staff who work with the technical team.

Our specialists provide essential help across system setup, troubleshooting, data management, event technology, and digital accessibility. At present, the support available to them varies between teams. Some volunteers have clear contacts and well-documented procedures, while others rely on informal knowledge or spend considerable time finding the right person to answer a question. The pilot is intended to test a more reliable approach without adding unnecessary administration.

Under the proposed model, participants will have:

- one named staff contact for operational questions;
- a shared library of current guides, checklists, and escalation paths;
- a fortnightly drop-in session for technical discussion and peer support;
- a simple process for requesting equipment, access, or specialist advice; and
- a short onboarding checklist for volunteers joining during the pilot.

We plan to invite up to 20 volunteers from a mix of roles, experience levels, and locations. Participation will be optional. The pilot will begin on 6 October and run until 19 December. We will review it at the halfway point and again at the end, using participant feedback, response times, unresolved request numbers, and volunteer retention as indicators.

We are particularly interested in your views on the following questions:

1. Which support problems take the most time or cause the most frustration now?
2. Are the proposed contact and drop-in arrangements practical for volunteers in different locations and time zones?
3. What guidance or resources should be included in the shared library first?
4. Could any part of the pilot create extra work, confusion, or access barriers?
5. What would make you confident that the program should continue after December?

After the feedback period closes, we will publish a summary of the main themes and explain any changes made to the pilot design. Invitations and the final schedule will follow the next week.

Thank you for helping us shape a program that is useful in practice. Our volunteer specialists are generous with both their time and expertise, and the support structure should respect that contribution.
