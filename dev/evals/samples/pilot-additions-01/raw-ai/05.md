Subject: Standards project update: progress, open issues, and August decisions

Hi team,

Here is this week’s status update for the interoperability standards project.

The drafting group has completed the revised core terminology and incorporated the comments accepted during the June review. The conformance examples are now aligned with draft 0.8, and the test team has validated 31 of the 36 required scenarios. We have also received written confirmation from three implementation partners that they can participate in the September trial.

Two issues remain unresolved. First, the groups still interpret the identifier-retention requirement differently when records move between systems. Legal and engineering are reviewing the latest wording, but we do not yet have language that both teams support. Second, five test scenarios depend on the reference dataset. The data group found licensing restrictions in the proposed source and is assessing two alternatives. Until that choice is made, those scenarios cannot be completed.

Over the next two weeks, we need decisions on:

- the identifier-retention rule and whether it belongs in the normative text or implementation guidance;
- the replacement reference dataset;
- the minimum pass threshold for the September trial; and
- whether draft 0.9 can proceed to external review with known limitations documented.

Decision papers will be circulated by 7 August. Please send material changes or missing risks to me by 5 August so they can be included. The steering meeting on 12 August will be used to settle the four points above and confirm the external-review timetable.

Thanks,

Rina
